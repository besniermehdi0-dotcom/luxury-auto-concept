      What this concept fixes
      
  
        
    
      1. Repair Status works. On the current site the menu link opens a "Page not found".
        2. 
    
      2. Before and after photos load. The three repair posts from 2019 show broken images.
        3. 
    
      3. A quote in under a minute. Customers tap the damaged panels and send the list on WhatsApp, already written.
        4. 
    
      4. One set of facts. The current pages say 10 years, 13 years, 16 stores and 3 garages. This uses your About page: open since 2006, Bosch Car Service since 2008, 3 garages.
        5. 
    
      5. Template leftovers gone. "Total listings", "Electric cars" and "Upcoming cars and events" came with the theme.
        6. 
    
      6. Built for phones. No page builder, no plugins, a fixed WhatsApp and call bar, fast on mobile data.
        7. 
  
      
  
      The workshop photographs here are licensed stand-ins. On the real site they are replaced with your own cars, bays and team.
