    (07)Questions
    
    
    Questions people ask first.
    
    
    
      
    Do I need a police report to claim?
    Yes. Insurers in the UAE ask for a police report before they approve an accident repair. Minor accidents can be reported in the Dubai Police app.
    
      
    My policy says agency repair. Can you still do it?
    Agency-repair policies usually send the car to the dealer. Send your policy on WhatsApp and we will tell you straight away whether we can take the claim.
    
      
    Can I pay for the repair myself?
    Yes. Send photos on WhatsApp and you get a price before any work starts.
    
      
    Which cars do you work on?
    Luxury, European, American and classic cars, including Porsche, Range Rover, Mercedes-Benz and BMW.
    
      
    Which parts and paint do you use?
    Factory-specified parts and the highest-quality paints, matched to your car's paint code.
    
      
    What are your opening hours?
    Monday to Saturday, 9 AM to 7 PM. Closed on Sunday.
